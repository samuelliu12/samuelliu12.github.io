Roller Coaster Modeling and Simulation

software

Summary

A roller coaster designed and simulated entirely in SolidWorks. The cart and track were modelled as separate parts and combined into an assembly, then run through SolidWorks physics-based motion simulation to check that the ride stays within safe speed and acceleration limits along the whole track.

What I did

  • Used SolidWorks to design a roller coaster assembly, including cart and track.
  • Ran physics simulations in SolidWorks to analyze the motion of the cart along the track, extracting speed and lateral/vertical acceleration profiles.

Results

  • Top speed of roughly 30 mph along the track.
  • Maximum lateral acceleration of about 0.4 g and maximum vertical acceleration of about 1.7 g, within typical rider-safety limits.
