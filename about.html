I'm an engineering student specialising in robotics and mechatronics, with a focus on embedded systems and control. I enjoy working across the full stack of physical systems — from PCB layout and mechanical design through to firmware and real-time control loops.

Most of my projects involve microcontrollers, sensors, and actuators. I write primarily in C and C++, and reach for Python when I need tooling, data analysis, or simulation. I'm comfortable with ROS 2, FreeRTOS, and a range of serial protocols including CAN, SPI, I2C, and UART.
